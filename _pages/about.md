I am a Master of Financial Mathematics student at MIT and a Research Assistant in Prof. Tomaso Poggio's group at MIT CSAIL / BCS. I am applying to PhD programs in Operations Research and Statistics for Fall 2027.

My research is about how training choices shape what models learn. In [*Same Error, Different Function*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.02620), we show that neural networks with near-identical test error can implement measurably different functions, and that the optimizer acts as an implicit prior. I am now studying agent skills and harnesses through the lens of optimization, drawing parallels between learning in weight space and in prompt space. With Prof. Georgia Perakis at the MIT Operations Research Center, I work on selecting context in multi-turn LLM conversations as a budget-constrained decision problem.

Before MIT, I earned a BSc in International Economics and Finance from Bocconi University (110/110 cum laude). I have also worked on quantitative research at Statkraft Trading and Quantbot Technologies, and as a founding machine learning engineer at DeepTree.

### Research interests

- Learning dynamics and implicit bias of optimization in deep networks
- Optimization of LLM agents: skills, harnesses, and prompts
- Decision-making under budget constraints for LLM systems
- Statistical learning theory
- Machine learning for financial time series
